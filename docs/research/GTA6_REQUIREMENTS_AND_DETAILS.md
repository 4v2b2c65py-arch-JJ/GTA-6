# GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) — Requirements and Details

> Researched: 2026-11-13 (web sources). **PC version/PC specs are not officially announced by Rockstar/Take-Two as of this date; console-only details below are from official/primary sources.**

## Official Release & Platforms
- **Release date:** November 19, 2026 (console). (Rockstar/Take-Two pre-order announcement; PlayStation Store; Xbox)
- **Announced platforms:** PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro (enhanced), Xbox Series X|S. (Rockstar/Take-Two, PlayStation Store, Xbox)
- **PC:** **Not announced.** No PC version, no PC release date, no PC storefront/launcher requirement confirmed by Rockstar. (Rockstar/Take-Two have not named PC; reports/rumors exist but are unconfirmed.)
- **Previous-gen consoles:** Not playable on PS4. Xbox One can stream via Xbox Cloud Gaming (Series S graphics settings when streamed) per Xbox Wire (Nov 10, 2026).
- **Cloud/streaming:** Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S at launch (1:00 AM ET Nov 19, 2026). Rockstar/Xbox messaging does not confirm PC streaming day-one; no official PC/cloud-to-PC announcement.

## Editions & Pricing (announced)
| Edition | Price (US) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Standard | $79.99 | Single-player experience (base). UK reported ~£69.99. |
| Ultimate | $99.99 | Premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and action across Jason & Lucia’s story. UK reported ~£89.99. |

- **Pre-order bonuses (as announced):** All pre-orders/purchases before Nov 20, 2026 include Vintage Vice City Pack; digital pre-orders include a free month of GTA+ (redeemable for GTA V/other Rockstar titles in GTA+ Games Library, per announcement details).
- **Pre-orders:** Began June 25, 2026 (midnight local). Available via PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store, and select/global retailers.
- **Rating:** Not yet rated at time of announcements (ESRB pending per listings).

## Pre-load, Storage & Distribution
- **Digital pre-load:** Begins November 12, 2026 (local midnight for PlayStation per PS support/page; timing varies by platform/store—check official store). Xbox notes pre-install available.
- **Physical:** Physical version contains a **download code in box** (no disc), available starting Nov 12, 2026 to support pre-loading (per Take-Two/Rockstar announcements and reporting).
- **Storage (console guidance):** PlayStation guidance indicates clearing **~212 GB** (minimum noted) on PS5 for pre-load/launch; exact final install size not universally published as a single official spec—plan for 200+ GB free.

## Game Details (announced)
- **Setting:** State of Leonida (includes Vice City), modern day.
- **Protagonists:** Jason and Lucia.
- **Mode:** Single-player experience announced (no multiplayer modes announced in cited materials).
- **Platforms/features (PS5):** Offline play, 1 player, Remote Play supported, DualSense haptics/adaptive triggers/speaker, Tempest 3D audio, fast loading (SSD). PS5 Pro Enhanced.
