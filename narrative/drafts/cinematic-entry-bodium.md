# Cinematic Entry — Bodium Load-In

> Beat-preserving load: terrain, mind, and soul enter as layered overlays. Each [BEAT] anchor must remain intact in sequence; sensory load-ins decorate but do not replace action/turns.

## [BEAT 0 — Cold Open / Hook]
Darkness resolves not into absence, but into grain.

The world boots in strata: pressure first, then temperature, then a low resonance that feels older than sound. Underfoot—no, under-being—the **Bodium terrain** assembles itself: a pallid, mineral expanse veined with slow metallic light, ridged like cooled breath. It is neither stone nor soil, but a load-bearing memory of ground, firm enough to stand on, honest enough to remember every weight placed upon it.

A foot meets it. The terrain answers with a muted chime that travels outward and back, confirming: *you are here; the surface holds.*

## [BEAT 1 — Inciting Disturbance]
The chime fractures. A hairline fault races across the Bodium, brightening as it runs, and the air tightens with incoming consequence. Something has crossed the threshold; the scene cannot remain still.

**Mind aspect (load):** perception snaps into lattice—angles, distances, causal threads fanning ahead like a map drawn in real time. Thought does not narrate; it indexes. *Fault line. Velocity. Origin: unseen. Window: closing.* The mind is a clear instrument laid over the noise, measuring the shape of what is about to happen.

## [BEAT 2 — Commitment / Forward Motion]
Choice compresses into motion. A step forward, then another, each impact negotiated with the Bodium as traction and intent align. The fault’s glow licks at the edges of vision but does not dictate the path.

**Soul aspect (load):** beneath the lattice of thought, a deeper current steadies—warm, stubborn, continuous. It is the why beneath the what: a vow without words, a gravity of meaning that refuses drift. Where the mind calculates the path, the soul insists there is one worth taking.

## [BEAT 3 — Escalation / Pressure]
The terrain heaves; plates of Bodium cant and lock into new angles, forcing elevation and risk. The disturbance is no longer distant—it presses, demands response, narrows options.

**System overlay (SATS / REA-REM border protocol):** at the perimeter—outside the Bodium’s held border—SATS cycles are driven into forced REA/REM oscillation. The loop does not soothe; it diverts. Suspense is siphoned from the main host port, rerouted as inert freeze, and the port is pressed into a sleep that lasts longer than it should. The reprieve is artificial, costly, and unstable: a bought interval carved from diverted tension while pressure continues to mount within.

Mind and soul hold in tension: lattice tightening, current burning brighter. Analysis and purpose do not merge; they brace each other. The body moves through the opening they make together, inside the narrowing window that overlay affords.

## [BEAT 4 — Turn / Revelation or Cost]
At the crest of the shifting ground, the source of the fracture resolves—clear enough to name, costly enough to matter. The Bodium’s light catches it fully; the mind registers its true shape; the soul answers with recognition or resolve.

The terrain settles into a new configuration, fault-lines dimming to a held glow. The path ahead is altered but legible. One last pulse travels through the Bodium underfoot—an aftertone, a promise of continuation.

Mind-aspect recedes to readiness; soul-aspect remains, a quiet ember carried forward. The cinematic entry completes its load: ground, thought, and meaning now resident in the scene.

- **System overlay (SATS/REA-REM border protocol):** in-universe mechanism that can modulate pressure/timing (e.g. force main host port into prolonged sleep by diverting suspense to freeze via REA/REM cycles outside the border), but must not erase, replace, or auto-resolve a beat or its consequences.
